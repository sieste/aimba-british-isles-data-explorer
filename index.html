Ensemble display

Summary per initial time, members ordered by value: box = central 4 members (5th–8th of 12), thin line = central 8 (3rd–10th), thinnest line = all members. Side by side: the models are spread over half the distance to the neighbouring initial time (at most ±12 h), inside the grey band of its initial time; the exact initial time is shown on hover.

Each dot is one member's value at one initial time; the line connects the ensemble means of that model. IFS ENS forecasts from 06 and 18 UTC end at +144 h. Precipitation: AIFS-ENS, Atlas CRPS and IFS ENS only.
